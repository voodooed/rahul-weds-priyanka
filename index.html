A LITTLE ABOUT OUR MAITHIL TRADITIONS

Mithila — the land of Sita — celebrates a wedding over many days, with rituals sung by the women of the family and painted on its walls and floors. Legend has it that King Janak had the walls of his city painted for Sita's wedding: the beginning of Madhubani art.
